How to Automatically Extract Invoice Data from Gmail to Google Sheets (2026)

The Problem: Manual Invoice Entry Is a Time Sink

If you are a freelancer or a small business owner, you know the routine. Invoices land in your inbox. You open each email, find the invoice, and start reading through it line by line. Then you switch over to your spreadsheet and carefully type in the invoice number, the date, the vendor name, the total amount, and the due date. One invoice done. On to the next. It takes about 3 to 5 minutes per invoice, and mistakes happen more often than you would like. A wrong digit in the amount or a misread date can cause real headaches later when you are trying to reconcile your books.

Now do some quick math. If you receive 20 invoices a month and each one takes 5 minutes to enter, that is almost 2 hours of your time every single month spent on pure data entry. Two hours you could spend on actual work, talking to clients, or simply taking a break. And this does not even count the time you spend fixing errors afterwards. There has to be a better way to handle invoice data entry automation, and there is.

The Solution: Let AI Do the Typing for You

There is a free Google Sheets add-on called AutoInv that handles this automatically using AI. You paste invoice text into a cell in your spreadsheet, click a button, and AutoInv reads the invoice and fills in the invoice number, date, vendor, amount, and due date for you in seconds. It works with any invoice format and any language. No templates, no configuration, no technical knowledge needed. Just install and click. It is the simplest way to extract invoice data in Google Sheets without doing the work yourself.

But it gets even better. If you upgrade to the Pro plan, you can connect your Gmail account and set up auto-import. Just create a Gmail label like "invoices," apply it to incoming invoice emails, and AutoInv will check your inbox every hour and import new invoices into your spreadsheet with zero manual work. It is a complete Gmail invoice to spreadsheet pipeline that runs in the background while you focus on your business.

Step-by-Step: How to Set Up AutoInv

Step 1: Install AutoInv from the Google Workspace Marketplace

Go to the Google Workspace Marketplace and search for "AutoInv." Click "Install" and grant the permissions it needs. The installation takes about 30 seconds. You do not need to download anything to your computer. It works entirely inside Google Sheets.

Step 2: Open a Google Sheet and Launch AutoInv

Open any Google Sheet, or create a new one. Then go to the Extensions menu at the top, find AutoInv, and click Open. A sidebar will appear on the right side of your screen. This is where you control everything. You will see two tabs: one for manual extraction and one for auto-import.

Step 3: Paste Invoice Text and Click "Extract Invoice Data"

Copy the text from any invoice you have received, whether it is in an email, a document, or any other format. Paste that text into a cell in column A of your spreadsheet. You can paste multiple invoices in multiple rows if you want. Then click the "Extract Invoice Data" button in the AutoInv sidebar. Within a few seconds, columns B through F will be filled with the invoice number, date, vendor name, total amount, and due date. That is it. You just went from manual data entry to AI invoice extraction with one click.

Step 4 (Pro): Set Up Gmail Auto-Import

This step is for Pro users who want to automate invoice entry completely. In Gmail, create a label called something like "invoices." Apply this label to emails that contain invoices. You can even set up a Gmail filter to do this automatically. Then go back to AutoInv, open the Auto-Import tab in the sidebar, and select the Gmail label you created. Click Enable. From now on, AutoInv will check your Gmail every hour, find new emails with that label, extract the invoice data, and add it to your spreadsheet. You never have to open an invoice email again.

You paste this text into cell A2 of your spreadsheet and click "Extract Invoice Data." Here is exactly what AutoInv produces:

INV-2025-0421 2025-04-21 Office Supplies Co. 54.53 2025-05-21

The AI correctly identified the invoice number, the date it was issued, the vendor name, the total amount including tax, and the payment due date. It ignored the line items and pulled only the structured data you need for your records. This is exactly how a free invoice extractor should work: fast, accurate, and no effort on your part.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Manual extraction, where you paste invoice text and click the button, is completely free and will stay free forever. The Pro plan adds Gmail auto-import for $4.99 per month if you want fully hands-off automation.

Is my data safe?

Yes. AutoInv does not store your invoice data on any external server. All data stays in your Google Sheet, which you own and control. The AI processes the text and returns the result directly to your spreadsheet. You can read the full details in our privacy policy.

What languages does it support?

AutoInv supports any language. Spanish, English, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, and many more. The AI understands them all. You do not need to change any settings. Just paste the invoice text in whatever language it is written in, and AutoInv will extract the data correctly.

How accurate is it?

Very accurate. The AI is trained to understand a wide range of invoice formats and layouts. That said, we always recommend reviewing important data before acting on it, just as you would double-check any automated process. For the vast majority of invoices, the extraction is spot on.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Not at all. If you can install a Google Sheets add-on and click a button, you can use AutoInv. There is nothing to configure, no code to write, and no formulas to learn. Install and click. That is it.

Can it read PDF invoices?

Not yet. For now, you need to paste the text from the invoice into the spreadsheet. You can copy text from a PDF, an email, or any other source. Direct PDF support is on our roadmap and is planned for a future update.
